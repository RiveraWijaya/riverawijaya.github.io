About

Toronto, Canada
Open to FPGA / RTL / embedded opportunities

I am an Electrical Engineering student at the University of Toronto focused on FPGA/RTL design, embedded systems, computer architecture, and the analog hardware that supports them. My projects span streaming datapaths, protocol bridges, DSP, RF power amplification, and power regulation.

I care about more than making a block compile. I want to know how it is verified, whether timing closes, how the interface fails, what the oscilloscope says, and whether another engineer can understand the design quickly.
